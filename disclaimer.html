1. Information only; not official

SafeNaksha is an independent awareness project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated on behalf of the National Crime Records Bureau, the Ministry of Home Affairs, any State Government or police force, any Commission for Women, or any government app or scheme with a similar name (e.g. "Raj Mahila Suraksha", "Suraksha", "NAKSHA"). Outlet names and logos belong to their owners and do not imply endorsement.

2. Statistics are relative, not a judgement on any place or people

District colours split all districts into three equal groups (lower, middle, higher third) for the chosen crime head and measure. This is a statistical, relative ranking of registered cases. Higher numbers can reflect larger population, better police registration, more awareness or reporting, or boundary changes — not only more crime. No colour or count is a statement about the character, safety, morality, caste, religion, community or residents of any district, city or locality, and must not be used to stigmatise them.

3. Media reports are unverified by us

  • Markers summarise what a published news article or public post reported, with links. We do not investigate or confirm incidents. Reports can be inaccurate, incomplete, later corrected, retracted or overtaken by court outcomes. Social-media items are marked unverified.
  • Any allegation against any person is only an allegation; every accused person is presumed innocent until proven guilty by a competent court.
  • Crime type, status ("FIR filed", "arrest reported") and place are assigned automatically from keywords and may be wrong. Always read the original source.
  • Pins are approximate (±2–5 km for a locality/police-station area, ±10–30 km for a city/district) and never mark the exact scene, a home or a person.

4. No guarantee of safety

No markers, few reports or a light-coloured district does NOT mean an area is safe. Do not rely on the Service for decisions affecting your or anyone's safety. Stay alert, trust your instincts, and in any emergency call 112.

5. Victim identity and children

We do not publish, and take active steps to remove, any information that could identify a survivor of a sexual offence or a child (BNS Sec. 72 & 73; POCSO Sec. 23; JJ Act Sec. 74; Nipun Saxena v. Union of India). Automated filters may miss something — if you see any such detail, please report it immediately and we will hide it at once.
