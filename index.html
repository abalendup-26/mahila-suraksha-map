Disclaimer

Yeh data adhoora hai (this data is incomplete). NCRB counts only crimes registered with police; many crimes are never reported. News/social-media markers cover only a small sample of recent reports picked up by automated search, and their locations are approximate (city/district or police-station area — never exact addresses).
No markers or a green district does NOT mean an area is safe. Higher counts can also reflect larger population or better reporting. Social-media items are unverified. Use this as awareness only, not as a guarantee. No victim identities are stored or shown (BNS Sec. 72).

Sources

• District data: NCRB, Crime in India 2024 (district-wise Crime Against Women tables; also 2022 & 2023), via Esri India Living Atlas layer “NCRB/District_Wise_Crime_Against_Women_2022” (link). District boundaries from the same layer.
• *Rate uses Census 2011 female population (latest census; districts created after 2011 or split since show no rate, and split parents may be under-stated).
• Incidents: Google News RSS search (English & Hindi) and public X posts / X news stories, collected . Locations geocoded with OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
