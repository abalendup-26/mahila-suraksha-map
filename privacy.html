2. Data we do NOT collect

We do not run a server-side application, user accounts, sign-up forms, cookies for tracking, analytics, advertising or fingerprinting. We do not collect your name, phone number, email, contacts or device identifiers through the Service.

3. Your location (DPDP Act, 2023)

  • Location is requested only when you tap "My location", through your browser's permission prompt. You can refuse; the map works without it (search a place instead).
  • If you allow it, your coordinates are processed only inside your browser, in memory, to centre the map, find your district and count reported incidents within 25 km. They are never sent to us, never stored (not in cookies, localStorage or any server) and are discarded when you close or reload the page.
  • Because we do not receive or store your location, we do not act as a Data Fiduciary for it. Your browser/OS handles the permission; you can revoke it in your browser settings at any time.
  • Map tiles for the area you view are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which therefore see your IP address and the map area requested (see Section 5).

5. Third-party services your browser contacts

To display the Service your browser connects directly to the following providers, which receive your IP address, browser details and the specific request, and process them under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages (hosting; GitHub, Inc.) — GitHub Privacy Statement. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security.
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers and Nominatim geocoding (OpenStreetMap Foundation) — the text you type in Search is sent to Nominatim. OSMF Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts (fonts) — Google Privacy Policy.
  • unpkg.com (Leaflet map library files; served via Cloudflare).
  • Links to news outlets / X open those third-party websites, which have their own policies.

6. Personal data inside the published content

  • The incident data is compiled from already-published news and public posts. Ages shown; names and identifying details removed. Adult ages are shown as published; for minor victims (under 18, or described as minor/नाबालिग/बच्ची) the age is replaced by “minor”/“नाबालिग”. We never store or show victims' names, names of family members, house numbers or street addresses, school/college/workplace names, phone numbers, photos or other identifying information (BNS Sec. 72; POCSO Sec. 23; JJ Act Sec. 74). These are removed by automated filters, and map locations are kept at locality/police-station or city level (never an exact address).
  • Headlines may mention accused persons, officials or places as published by the outlet. Anyone mentioned may ask for correction or removal (Section 8). Persons accused are presumed innocent unless convicted by a court.
  • Under the DPDP Act, personal data that a person has made, or caused to be made, publicly available may fall outside the Act's scope; nonetheless we will consider requests for correction or erasure from any person concerned.
